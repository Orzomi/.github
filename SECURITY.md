# Security

## Reporting a problem
If you find a security problem in anything Orzomi makes, email **info@orzomi.com**. Please don't open a public issue, and don't share it publicly before we have fixed it.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it.

## What happens next
- We reply within 3 working days.
- We keep you updated while we fix it, and tell you when it's fixed.
- If you'd like, we credit you once the fix is out.

## Good faith
We won't take legal action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith, doesn't access or change other people's data beyond what's needed to show the problem, and gives us reasonable time to fix it.
